# Apple Polish

**Look:** quiet, physical and instantly responsive. The interface gets out of the way, and everything you touch feels like a real object.
**Good for:** app screens, native-feeling web apps, gesture-driven UI, sheets and drawers.

## Response and touch

- **Respond the instant a finger lands.** Highlight on press, not on release; any delay on the input path feels dead.
- **Track the finger 1:1** during drags, sliders and sheets, and keep going with the gesture's speed when it's released.
- **Everything can be interrupted.** A user can grab or reverse anything mid-animation; nothing locks out input while it moves.
- **Springs, not timed curves,** for anything the user moves, so motion keeps its momentum.

## Space and material

- **Things come from where they were triggered and leave the way they came.** A menu grows from its button; a panel that slid in from the right leaves to the right.
- **Depth shows hierarchy.** Translucent, layered bars and sheets over content, with heavier materials for structure and lighter ones for what you touch; dim the background for a modal task.
- **Soft edges instead of hard dividers** where content scrolls under floating chrome.

## Type and clarity

- **The system font first,** with tracking and line height tuned per size: tighter on large headings, looser on body.
- **Hierarchy from weight, size and spacing together,** not size alone.
- **Respect the user's text size;** layouts grow with it instead of breaking.
- **Every screen answers where am I, where can I go, how do I get out,** and labels name what's there ("Library", not "Home").

## Accessibility

- **Reduced motion replaces slides and springs with short cross-fades; reduced transparency makes glass solid.**
