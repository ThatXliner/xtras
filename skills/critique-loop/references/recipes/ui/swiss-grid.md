# Swiss Grid

**Look:** rational, restrained and objective. A strict grid, neutral type and plenty of white space; the content does the talking.
**Good for:** editorial sites, portfolios, dashboards, documentation, posters, information-heavy pages.

## Structure

- **Start from a grid and keep to it.** Every element aligns to columns and a baseline; an element placed "by eye" off the grid is a fail.
- **Asymmetric, not centred.** Layouts are arranged off-centre, with tension coming from proportion rather than decoration.
- **White space is a material.** Leave room; it separates and ranks content better than boxes and lines do.

## Type

- **Neutral sans-serif type** (grotesques like Helvetica, Univers, Akzidenz-Grotesk, or a modern equivalent), used as a vehicle for the words, not decoration.
- **Flush left, ragged right.** No centred or justified body text.
- **Hierarchy from size, weight and position,** with very few sizes, not from colour or ornament.

## Restraint

- **Objective, not expressive.** Present the information clearly; persuasion through style, gimmicks or mood is out of place.
- **Photography over illustration** where an image is needed, as the more direct record.
- **Reduce until it breaks, then add one thing back.** Anything that doesn't carry content goes: shadows, gradients, rounded cards, icons for their own sake.
- **The design comes from the content,** not from a style applied on top.
