# Bright Explainer

**Look:** carefully sourced ideas turned into one visual metaphor per scene, in bright flat illustration with a score made for the piece.
**Good for:** science and civic explainers, educational shorts, product explainers.
**Source:** distilled from Kurzgesagt's published description of their process ([kurzgesagt.org/youtube](https://kurzgesagt.org/youtube/)). Not endorsed by them.

## Truth first

- **Every fact traces to a primary source.** Papers, books and experts in the field, collected on a source sheet for the piece. A claim the critic can't trace is a fail, however good it looks.
- **Simplify without saying anything false.** Leaving out detail is fine; a simplification that becomes untrue is not.

## Script

- **The script is the backbone; the visuals serve it.** Expect many drafts. Cut anything that doesn't move the idea forward, and balance facts with a story the viewer can follow.

## Visuals

- **One visual metaphor per idea, sketched before it's drawn.** A scene that only shows text, or illustrates the words literally, misses the point of animation.
- **Bright, rounded, flat 2D, with 3D only where it helps.** Consistent palette and shapes across the piece.
- **Recurring characters** give a series identity. Keep them on model from scene to scene.

## Sound and timing

- **The narration sets the timing.** Animation follows the voice, so emphasis lands on the right picture.
- **Score each piece.** Music written or chosen for this video's mood, with sound effects that support it. A generic library track that could sit under any video is a fail.
