# Interface Words

**Look:** the words are the interface. Short labels in the customer's language, with one deliberate personality throughout.
**Good for:** UI microcopy, buttons, empty states, onboarding, settings, notifications.

## Copy is design

- **Treat every label, count and message as a design decision,** reviewed like layout and colour, not filled in at the end.
- **Write with real copy from the start.** Mockups with lorem ipsum or fake names hide problems until it's too late.

## Short, in their words

- **Say what the reader needs and stop.** If a line can lose half its words, it should.
- **Use the customer's word, not the team's.** No internal names, acronyms or engineer-to-engineer phrasing.
- **Buttons name the action:** "Save", "Update", "Create invoice", never a generic "Submit" or "OK" when something specific happens.

## One personality

- **Decide the product's personality** (stern, funny, modest, warm) and check every new line against it. A joke in one corner and corporate speak in the next reads as two products.
