# Plain & Friendly

**Look:** copy that sounds like a smart, kind person talking: clear first, funny only when it's easy, never selling harder than the truth.
**Good for:** product copy, marketing pages, emails, release notes, captions and video scripts.
**Source:** distilled from the [Mailchimp Content Style Guide](https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone/) (CC BY-NC 4.0, © Mailchimp) and common lists of AI writing tells. Not endorsed by Mailchimp.

## Voice

- **Clear beats entertaining.** If a joke or metaphor makes the point harder to get, it goes.
- **Humour is dry and optional:** winking, not shouting. A forced joke is worse than none; when unsure, play it straight.
- **Smart but not snobbish, informal but not sloppy.** Never condescending; bring the reader in.
- **Tone follows the reader's state.** Someone stuck or worried gets calm and direct, not playful.

## Truth

- **No hype.** Grandiose claims, dramatic storytelling, over-promises and upsells are a fail. Name the real strength instead.
- **No invented proof** ("trusted by thousands") and nothing that isn't true yet.

## Mechanics

- **Active voice, plain English, specific words.** Cut fluff and jargon.
- **Calls to action say what happens** ("Start your free trial", not "Get started").

## AI tells to catch

- **Stock words:** delve, tapestry, seamless, unlock, elevate, leverage, "in today's fast-paced world".
- **Stock shapes:** "it's not X, it's Y", everything in threes, a run of short punchy sentences, a one-line paragraph as a punchline.
